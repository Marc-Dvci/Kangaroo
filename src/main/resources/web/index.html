Who is checking this baby?

Kangaroo follows the World Health Organization's newborn danger-sign protocol. It works with no signal and no internet connection.

How this works, and what it cannot do

Kangaroo runs entirely on this device. Photographs are analysed here and are not stored after the assessment. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you explicitly allow it.

It cannot examine a baby. It reads what you tell it and what a camera can see, applies the WHO protocol, and tells you what that protocol says. It never says a baby is fine — it says whether anything it can see needs a clinician.

Decision support only. It does not replace clinical judgement. Technical details

The check

    About the baby
    Sex
    What is worrying you?

    Use your own words. Say what changed, and when it started.

    Breathing

    Watch the baby's chest for a full minute while they are calm. Tap once for every breath.

    60s

    Optional: point the camera at the baby's chest for fifteen seconds and Kangaroo will measure the rate itself, as a second opinion on the count.

    No video is sent or stored. Each frame becomes a single movement number on this device.

    Photographs

    Hold the printed colour card beside the baby's skin, in the best light you have.

    Photographs are analysed on this device and are not kept after the assessment.

    The cry

    Ten seconds of the baby's cry, with the phone held comfortably close. A weak, absent or high-pitched cry is a danger sign.

    10s

    The recording is analysed on this device. If it cannot be graded, Kangaroo says so rather than guessing.

    Privacy

    When this is on, Kangaroo will not use a cloud model even if one is configured.
